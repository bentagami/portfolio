I created a platform for visitors to make their own rover and test it out in our roverscape. I made a standardized box that visitors could attach different wheel designs to and press a button to turn it on. Dwell time was very high and visitors loved the prototyping process and seeing which wheels would preform better than others. ![](/images/projects/IMG_20250730_122213.jpg)

This was a really quick project and a good demonstration of the ethos of our Executive Director at the time. We were focused on incredibly quick turnaround times (this from design to implementation was \~2 weeks), and less focused on fit and finish. It helped I facilitated the exhibit the whole time, but I really got to see how engaging this was for visitors from \~3 all the way to \~90. The activity was a easy way to give an excuse to make something. I loved seeing parents start parallel playing alongside their kids - just as focused on making their rover.
