Hi, I'm Ben! 

I love making! I have a degree in Materials Science and Engineering, and work experience in the Semiconductor Industry as a Process Engineer, Additive Manufacturing as a Test Engineer, and in the Museum Industry as an Engineer-in-Residence. 

I take engineering concepts and turn them into reliable hardware. I'm comfortable across the full creation spectrum: I enjoy designing, fabricating and testing. I have experience with CAD, 3D printing, CNC machining, fabrication, electronics integration, and test design. I love to rapidly prototype, and create things that work and break and study why they break. I'm experienced selecting materials for performance and environmental exposure, designing under tight constraints, and collaborating with both engineers and non-engineering partners to get a product working and ready to go.
